My Story 我的故事

How I Learned to Notice 我如何开始观察

This page is not a resume. It is the path from childhood experience, family values, and classroom practice to the questions I now study. 这一页不是简历，而是从童年经历、家庭教育和课堂实践，走向我现在研究问题的路径。

01

Where I started 我从哪里开始

Between a village hometown and an urban childhood. 在村庄老家和城市成长之间。

My hometown is a village in Beijing, but I grew up in the city. Because of that, the urban-rural gap was never only a concept I read about; it was something I could feel across places. 我的老家在北京的一个村子里，但我从小在城市里长大。因此，城乡差距对我来说不是只在书上看到的概念，而是我在不同地方之间真实感受到的东西。

02

Family values 家庭教育

Education should also mean giving back. 教育也应该意味着回馈。

My parents wanted me to see a larger world and experience better education so I could keep growing. But they also taught me that after seeing more, I should not forget where I came from, or the people and places I may one day be able to help. 爸妈希望我去看更大的世界、感受更好的教育，是为了让我不断提升自己。但他们也告诉我，见过更大的世界之后，不能忘记自己的来处，也不能忘记未来有能力时可以回馈的人和地方。

03

Family tradition 家族传统

Giving back was something people practiced. 回馈是家里一直在做的事。

I also grew up hearing how older generations in my family tried to contribute to the village when they had the ability. That made giving back feel less like a grand statement and more like a practical habit: if you have received more, you should look for ways to help where you can. 我也从小听到家里祖辈在有能力的时候为村里做贡献。这让我觉得回馈不是一个宏大的口号，而是一种很实际的习惯：当一个人获得了更多，也应该在力所能及的时候看看自己能帮到哪里。

04

What I began to notice 我开始注意到什么

The gap became visible in classrooms. 差距在课堂里变得具体。

When I later taught at the primary school near my hometown, the differences I had sensed growing up became visible in classroom resources, teaching conditions, student confidence, and teacher workload. 后来我回到老家附近的小学支教，小时候感受到的差异在课堂资源、教学条件、学生信心和教师工作量里变得更具体。

05

What I am still learning 我仍在学习什么

Care needs evidence, methods, and humility. 关心需要证据、方法和谦逊。

These experiences pushed me to study education finance, economics, public policy, and school systems. I am still learning how to listen before proposing solutions, and how to keep ideas connected to the people they hope to serve. 这些经历推动我继续研究教育财政、经济学、公共政策和学校体系。我仍在学习如何先倾听再提出方案，也在学习如何让想法始终连接到它想帮助的人。
