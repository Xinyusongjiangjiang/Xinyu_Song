On Snow

A place for skiing videos, action photos, and the kind of attention I feel when my body has to learn balance, speed, and recovery.

A Reset

When school feels crowded, skiing gives me a way to release pressure. The cold air, movement, and need to focus leave less room for the noise I carry with me.

Agency

On a slope, I cannot control the weather or the terrain. I can choose my line, adjust my speed, and decide when to slow down. That small, physical sense of agency matters to me.

Practice

Skiing has taught me that control is not forcing every outcome. It is paying attention, responding well, and trusting myself to begin again after a fall.
