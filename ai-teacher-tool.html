AI Support Tool for Teachers 教师 AI 支持工具

This page is about a small tool idea for making differentiated teaching easier to prepare and more practical to sustain.这一页关注一个小工具想法：让分层教学更容易准备，也更容易持续实践。

01

Observation 观察

Teachers value differentiation, but time is limited. 老师认可分层教学，但时间和资源有限。

Many teachers see the value of differentiated instruction, but limited time, materials, and support often make it difficult to carry out in real classrooms.我注意到，很多老师认可分层教学的价值，但因为时间、材料和支持资源不足，很难在真实课堂中持续推进。

classroom / teacher need
课堂或教师需求
02

Design question 设计问题

A tool should support both teachers and students. 工具应该同时支持老师和学生。

How can a small AI tool help teachers prepare differentiated materials more efficiently, while helping students learn in ways that better fit their needs?一个小型 AI 工具，如何帮助老师更高效地准备分层材料，同时也帮助学生用更适合自己的方式学习？

prototype / workflow
原型或流程
03

Why it matters 为什么重要

Good teaching should be easier to repeat. 好的教学方式应该更容易持续发生。

Differentiated teaching should not depend only on whether one teacher has extra time. Better tools may help good teaching become more practical and repeatable.分层教学不应该只取决于某位老师有没有额外时间。更合适的工具，可能让好的教学方式更容易被实践和持续使用。

feedback / classroom use
反馈或课堂使用
