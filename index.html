Where My Questions Begin 我的问题从哪里开始

Classrooms are where my questions begin: where I learn as a student, and where I teach and observe as a volunteer. Majors and interests are how I organize those questions through economics, public policy, and education. Life is what keeps those questions connected to a real person. 课堂是我问题的起点：一边是我作为学生学习的课堂，另一边是我作为志愿者支教和观察的小学课堂。专业与兴趣帮助我用经济学、公共政策和教育来组织这些问题。生活则让这些问题始终和真实的人连接在一起。

My Story 我的故事

My story begins between two places: a village hometown in Beijing and the city where I grew up. That distance shaped how I noticed education, responsibility, and the meaning of returning to help. 我的故事开始于两个地方之间：北京的村庄老家，和我从小长大的城市。这样的距离，让我开始理解教育、责任，以及回到一个地方去帮助别人的意义。

Childhood 小时候

Between a Village Hometown and an Urban Childhood 在村庄老家和城市成长之间

My hometown is a village in Beijing, but I grew up in the city. Moving between these two worlds made the urban-rural gap feel personal rather than abstract. 我的老家在北京的一个村子里，但我从小在城市里长大。往返于这两个世界之间，让城乡差距对我来说不是抽象概念，而是真实感受。

Family education 家庭教育

Learning to Give Back 学会回馈家乡和帮助别人

My parents wanted me to see a larger world and experience better education so I could keep growing. But they also taught me that after seeing more, I should not forget where I came from. 爸妈希望我去看更大的世界、感受更好的教育，是为了让我不断提升自己。但他们也告诉我，见过更大的世界之后，不能忘记自己的来处和家乡。

Family tradition 家族传统

A Habit of Contributing When Able 有能力时为村里做点事

I also grew up hearing how older generations in my family tried to contribute to the village when they had the ability. Giving back was not presented as a slogan, but as something people do when they are able to help. 我也从小听到家里祖辈在有能力的时候为村里做贡献。回馈不是一句口号，而是一种在自己有能力时尽力帮一帮的习惯。

Teaching practice 支教实践

Returning to Teach at My Hometown Primary School 回到老家的小学支教

When I later taught at the primary school near my hometown, the differences I had sensed growing up became visible in classrooms: in resources, teaching conditions, and what students could receive every day. 后来我回到老家附近的小学支教，小时候感受到的差异变得更具体：它出现在课堂资源、教学条件，以及学生每天能接触到的学习支持里。
