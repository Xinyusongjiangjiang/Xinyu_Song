Education Finance & Opportunity 教育财政与机会

I use economics to ask why students in different places may have very different learning conditions, even when they all have access to school.我用经济学追问：为什么学生都有学上，但不同地区的学习条件仍然可能很不一样。

01

Observation 观察

Access is not the same as opportunity. 有学上，不等于拥有相近的机会。

Education inequality is not only about whether students can attend school. It is also about whether different places can offer comparable teachers, materials, support, and learning conditions.教育不平等不只是学生有没有学上，也包括不同地区是否能提供相对接近的教师、材料、支持和学习条件。

02

Economic lens 经济学视角

Resources travel through systems. 资源不是自动抵达学生的。

How do local fiscal capacity, public spending, teaching methods, and school systems shape the quality of education students actually receive?地方财政能力、公共支出、教育方式和学校体系，如何共同影响学生真正获得的教育质量？

03

Policy question 政策问题

Fairness may mean raising the floor. 公平也许首先意味着提高底线。

I do not expect every region to have identical resources, but I want to understand what kinds of policy, economic tools, and education-system changes can raise the floor for students in less-developed areas.我不认为每个地区都能拥有完全一样的教育资源，但我想理解什么样的政策、经济手段和教育体系调整，可以提高欠发达地区学生的教育底线。
