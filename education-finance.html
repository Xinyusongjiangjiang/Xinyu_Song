Education Finance & Opportunity

I use economics to examine why students in different places can have very different learning conditions, even when they all have access to school. By studying fiscal capacity, public spending, resource allocation, and school systems, I want to understand how policy and economic tools can raise the floor for students in less-developed areas.

Project

Education Finance Research Project

This interdisciplinary project combines economics and mathematics to examine how regional fiscal disparities shape educational inequality in China. It connects economic reasoning about public resources and fiscal equalization with mathematical analysis, bringing together a final presentation and supporting calculus work.
